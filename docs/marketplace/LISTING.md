## Description

The field takes Markdown. Between 250 and 1,500 characters, in English; the first 250 must
be unformatted, because they become the search snippet (Elgato's
[product guidelines](https://docs.elgato.com/guidelines/products/)). Paste everything inside
the block (1,469 characters).

```markdown
Control your smart home from the Stream Deck. Lights, sockets, wall switches, blinds and scenes from devices on the Tuya® or Smart Life® platform, the ones already in your Smart Life or Tuya Smart® app, go on your keys, answering in about 100 ms over your own network.

**What it does**
- One key per output: a 4-gang wall switch becomes four keys
- Live state on every key, in sync with the phone, with watts where measured
- Blinds open, close, stop or nudge, and the key draws where the blind is
- Scenes from your app, or recorded on the Deck
- Hold **All off** to switch off everything at once
- Cloud fallback for devices that refuse local control
- 14 icons, 9 colours, your own label; panel in English, Portuguese and Spanish

**Setup, inside the panel**
1. Drag a Tua Casa action onto a key.
2. Type your app's user code, then scan the QR code with the app.
3. Pick a device. Python is bundled: nothing else to install.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11, Stream Deck 7.1 or later, devices set up in the app and the PC on their network.

**Good to know:** pairing uses the app identity of Home Assistant's Tuya integration. If Tuya changes it, pairing stops until an update; devices on your network keep working.

Tuya, Smart Life and Tuya Smart are registered trademarks of their respective owners; this plugin is not affiliated with them. Open source under GPL-3.0, built on open-source libraries credited in the README: https://github.com/ffapd1989/tuacasa
```

## Demo video

The reviewer has no Tuya devices, so a video of real keys driving real devices is the only
proof they will have that the plugin works. The only video slot in the submission is a
gallery item, so it goes there, next to the images. MP4, 1920×1080, **under 50 MB**.

The shots are the ones in [LAUNCH.md](../LAUNCH.md#demo-video), and the same footage serves
both. What the store version adds:

- The gallery is public, so the narration speaks to whoever is installing, not to the
  reviewer.
- Panel in English and English captions, because the reviewer reads English.
- Nothing readable that identifies an account or a house: no user code, no QR, no device
  IDs or local keys from the Activity log. Blur them in the edit.

The current video runs 1:07 and weighs 8 MB; it is not versioned, because the repository is
no place for it. It was recorded live in the Stream Deck app on the author's Stream Deck XL,
with the narration from OpenAI's `gpt-4o-mini-tts`. What it shows, in order:

1. The title card.
2. A light key tapped on and off twice: the key lights up when the device reports back.
3. A blind closed, stopped and opened, with the blind key following the motor.
4. A scene from the app, then All off held: every lit key goes dark.
5. The panel and the pairing screens, from the gallery images.
6. The closing card, with the repository and the trademark notice.

## Before pressing submit

- [ ] The Maker Agreement read (Maker Console → the organisation's Settings), looking for
      anything about third-party services and about licences
- [ ] The name "Tua Casa" still free on Marketplace
- [ ] `npx streamdeck validate com.felipedrummond.tuacasa.sdPlugin` and `npm run pack` on
      the commit being sent; the package is about 19 MB, so check it fits the upload limit
- [ ] The first upload goes with *Publish after review* unticked
- [ ] The protected build downloaded from the Versions tab and installed in place of the
      linked one (`npx streamdeck unlink com.felipedrummond.tuacasa` first). It passes when
      the log shows `worker up` and `cloud connected`, a key switches a device, and no
      `__pycache__` folder appears under the plugin's `python/`
- [ ] The name cannot be changed after submission without writing to maker@elgato.com
